# Terminology

DADE should use the same words consistently. This file is the current working vocabulary.

## DADE

Digital Asset Deed Exchange.

The technology and standard being developed by Pajic for digital ownership.

Use **DADE** for the current project. **DADE 1** can be used when a version number is useful.

## DADE 2

A reserved name for a possible future industry standard with native support from major operating system, hardware, storefront, and platform companies.

DADE 2 is not the current implementation.

## Digital deed

The consumer-facing ownership object.

A digital deed proves that a DADE identity owns a digital asset and records the rights attached to that ownership.

In normal product language, prefer **digital deed** or simply **deed** instead of protocol terms.

## DADE identity

The cryptographic identity that owns deeds.

A DADE identity is not the same thing as a Pajic account, store account, email address, legal name, or device.

A person can link those things to their DADE identity for convenience, but the deed belongs to the identity itself.

## Owner

The DADE identity that currently holds a deed.

A device using the product is not automatically the owner.

## Issuer

The company or other party that issues a deed for a product.

For the first Pajic game, Pajic would be the issuer.

## Asset

The digital product or right represented by a deed.

Examples include a game, software title, album, movie, book, plugin, font, or another digital product.

## Rights

The things the owner is allowed to do under the deed.

Examples include offline use, installation on personal devices, lending, gifting, resale, and transfer.

## Device authorization

Permission for a device to use an asset owned by a DADE identity.

Device authorization does not transfer ownership to the device.

## Transfer

A change in ownership from one DADE identity to another.

Selling and gifting are both forms of transfer.

## Loan

Temporary use by another person while the original owner keeps the underlying ownership right.

The exact technical rules for lending are not final yet.

## Ownership proof

Cryptographic evidence that a DADE identity currently owns a specific asset.

The final proof format is still being designed.

## Store account

An account used by a seller or platform for purchases, downloads, updates, multiplayer, cloud features, support, and similar services.

A store account can be linked to a DADE identity. It is not the ownership object itself.

## Permanent ownership

A purchase intended to remain usable without periodic license renewal or a continuing subscription.

If the underlying product depends on a live online service, that dependency must be stated clearly.

## Title credential

A technical term for the cryptographic credential used to prove or exercise ownership.

This is implementation language. It should normally stay out of consumer-facing screens.
